Our Story

A little corner of Hà Nội in Footscray

Cô Đào means "Auntie Đào" — and that is exactly the welcome we set out to give. Every bowl is built on a family recipe carried from Vietnam: pork marinated overnight, grilled over open charcoal, and finished with herbs picked that morning.

Tucked inside Footscray Market, our room is small, bright and full of life. Sit a while, slow down, and let the smell of the grill do the talking.
